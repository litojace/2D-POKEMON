# Equipped circular player portraits

Player identity uses one renderer (`framedAvatar` in `portraits.ts`) in the account
header, PvP and spectator lobbies, raid parties, raid results, Friends, and Nearby
player profile cards. Each renderer receives the equipped champion skin and
awakening stage, Colors and frame, plus verified boss-clear counts. Champion skins
take priority over Colors, just as they do on the battle rig. Full fighter models,
roster cards and item-only cosmetic previews keep their respective layouts.

The inner `portrait-disc` clips artwork to a circle. The outer circular frame
stays unclipped so its ornaments, rarity edge and earned stars remain visible.
All frame item previews are circular as well. Small identity widgets use a still
portrait with the same CSS halo, rays and orbiting sparks as the fitting room;
they do not allocate particle canvases. Sparks keep their full circular shape
throughout their orbit. Frame stars
use the existing progression: one per ten clears, capped at five; Legendary at
three and Mythical at five.

The Candy (Cookie), Garden (Vine), Festival (Tassel) and Winter (Icicle) frames
are scalable SVG artwork in `collection-frames.css`, generated by
`scripts/frame-art.mjs`: a chocolate-chip cookie with dripping icing, sprinkles
and a bonbon; twisting vines with leaves, blossoms and a large bloom; red lacquer
with a gold lattice, coin studs and a hanging knot and tassel; and faceted ice
with a snow cap, icicles and a snowflake crest. Festival and Winter are both Epic,
so their turning edge and gems use each set's own colours (red/gold versus
ice blue) rather than the shared purple.

The account header and own lobby portrait update immediately from the saved
Wardrobe. Peers use server-authenticated appearance reads in `FighterAppearance`.
Raid reward saves refresh these portraits immediately, including a newly earned
star. A refresh loads wardrobe slots, champion skins and clear counts together,
ignores older
responses and departed rooms, preserve verified gear during provider failures,
and explicitly clear unequipped items. Appearance metadata survives ordinary
battle snapshots, including all aura/entrance/victory slots already consumed by
the gameplay renderer.

Friends use the live verified loadout when available. Missing live fields retain
the saved appearance; explicit clears replace it. Migration
`033_shared_portrait_appearance.sql` extends the friend-only projection with the
last chosen champion, its equipped skin/stage, wardrobe slots and victory counts,
so offline portraits match. Incoming/outgoing friend requests still expose only
names and IDs. No owned inventory is exposed. The authenticated
`arena_set_portrait_character` RPC accepts only the caller's valid champion choice;
server-side writes are serialized to preserve rapid selection order. The new
migration follows the existing account, Friends, Shop and raid migrations. It has
been applied to the configured Supabase project, with a verified authenticated-only
choice RPC.

Validation covers shared rendering and palette precedence, earned stars, stale
lookups, unequips, friend-only SQL access, real socket/REST propagation, and PC and
phone layouts with decorated circular frames.
