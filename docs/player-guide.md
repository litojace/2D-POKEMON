# Player guide

How the game plays, mode by mode: rooms, party rules, controls, raids, the Plaza, the Shop and missions. Moved here from the README (27 Nov 2026) so the README can stay short. These notes were written as each feature shipped, so a few numbers have moved on since (the Shop, for one, now has many more skins, in five rarities): where this page and the code on `main` disagree, the code wins. Back to the [README](../README.md).

## The fighters

Eighteen original creatures, two per element — **Voltkit** and **Coilemur** (Electric), **Cindrake** and **Pyranda** (Fire), **Tidefin** and **Glacelle** (Water), **Auralynx** and **Halcyra** (Aura), **Lumoth** and **Sunbara** (Light), **Mystowl** and **Astrabun** (Psychic), **Terradon** and **Wombolder** (Earth), **Duskveil** and **Shadopus** (Shadow), **Pangrove** and **Mossloth** (Nature). Each has one signature basic attack (no cooldown, server-enforced attack interval), four special skills on cooldowns, plus the universal dash and jump. Mobile controls put the basic attack on the large button with the four skills curving around it.

## Play

Create a private room, share its six-character code, select a fighter, and confirm Ready. Both players acknowledge the introduction before the countdown. Win three rounds to win the match (best of five). Solo practice has a respawning target, character switching, reset, unlimited cooldowns and optional target attacks.

**2v2 Teams:** choose _2v2 Teams_ before _Create private room_ and share the code with three friends. Players pick Blue or Red in the lobby (the host can _Shuffle teams_); the match starts when all four are ready. First team to win three rounds takes it. Attacks never hurt teammates; area attacks hit every enemy inside. A knocked-out player watches their partner until the round ends. If someone leaves mid-match, their partner plays on 2v1; if a whole team leaves, it forfeits. On a time-out the team with more health left (as a share of full health) wins the round.

**Free-for-all:** choose _Free-for-all_ and share the code with two or three friends. Everyone fights everyone; the last fighter standing takes the round (on a time-out, the healthiest still standing). It starts when everyone in the room is ready, with three or four players. Someone who leaves is out, and the match goes on until one fighter remains.

**Party rules:** the host of any room can switch on _Turbo_ (skills recharge twice as fast, ultimates charge 50% faster), _Glass cannon_ (+50% damage), _Orb rain_ (orbs twice as often), _Quick match_ (first to two), _Calm court_ (Coliseum Sands only, no hazards), _Crown Hill_ (hold the centre circle alone for 12 seconds to take the round), _Orb Rush_ (carry gems to your own goal; bank 5 to take the round, big hits knock gems loose), _Fighter roulette_ (a random fighter each round), _Double time_ (60% faster running) and _Giants_ (huge-looking fighters). Everyone readies up again after a change.

**Watching and friends:** enter a room code and choose _Watch this room instead_ to watch a match live without playing (up to six watchers). Menu → _Friends_ shows your 8-character friend code, friend requests and who's online, with _Join_ for open lobbies and _Watch_ for live matches; the result card offers _Add as a friend_ for the people you just played ([migration 005](../supabase/migrations/005_friends.sql)).

**EX skills:** press your ultimate before its meter is full (33% or more) to arm an EX version of your next skill: 35% more damage and 20% more reach for a third of the meter. Works the same on keyboard, controller and touch.

**2v2 team-ups:** partners within 260 px both deal 10% more damage, and each gives the other a gift from their element: faster basics (Electric), +6% damage (Fire), 2 health a second (Water), 8% less damage taken (Aura), faster skill recharge (Light), faster ultimate (Psychic), 40% less knockback (Earth), or +10% against slowed, burning or stunned foes (Shadow).

**2v2 tuning:** 2v2 has its own health and basic-attack speed per champion (`TEAM_TUNING`; duels and raids keep the champions' own numbers), re-tuned after the bot fixes: every champion's 2v2 team win rate is 48–55% in bot matches (with the old factors and the fixed bots it was 31–65%); tuned with `npx tsx scripts/balance/tune-teams.ts`, details in [docs/new-champions/README.md](new-champions/README.md#2v2).

**Free-for-all tuning:** free-for-all has its own health and basic-attack speed per champion too (`FFA_TUNING`, and `FFA_THREE_TUNING` for a free-for-all that started with three players), because the last fighter standing wins and melee champions, who take about twice the damage per second of ranged ones, last half as long. Re-tuned after the bot fixes: every champion wins 22–30% of four-player bot free-for-alls (fair is 25%; with the old factors and the fixed bots it was 10–53%) and 30–38% of three-player ones (fair is 33%; it was 25–41%); tuned with `npx tsx scripts/balance/tune-teams.ts --mode ffa [--players 3]`, measured with `npx tsx scripts/balance/measure-ffa.ts`, details in [docs/new-champions/README.md](new-champions/README.md#free-for-all).

**Bot difficulty:** Hard and Normal bots play every champion about equally well relative to the others, so a champion's win rate against bots no longer flips with difficulty (it used to: Cindrake won 27% of Hard bot duels and 74% of Normal ones); every champion's Hard–Normal gap is now under 8 points in bot duels, and with health and attack speed re-tuned for the fixed bots every champion wins 46.5–53.1% of bot duels (with the old numbers they ran 28–66% once Normal stopped flipping them). Normal bots are held back by a per-champion share of "dazed" seconds (`dazeBy` in `apps/server/src/bot.ts`) rather than by weaknesses that only hurt ranged champions. With several opponents, bots pick the nearest, most hurt one they can see instead of the first to join. Details in [docs/new-champions/README.md](new-champions/README.md#bots-hard-and-normal).

**Mastery flair:** reaching a mastery tier with a fighter (Bronze 5, Silver 15, Gold 30, Crystal 60 matches) puts a ring in that tier's colour under them in matches; the server checks it against your records.

**Every round:** in the last minute a ring closes from the whole court to the centre; outside it you lose 14 health a second. Orbs alternate between healing (green, +25% health) and energy (gold, +35% ultimate charge). In 2v2, partners close together get a team-up bonus (below).

**Your name:** your player card (your name in the top bar) has _Change name_. The first change can happen at any time; after that, once every 14 days, counted on the server's clock (the card says how long is left). A name is 1 to 20 characters with at least one letter or number, without control or invisible characters; spaces are tidied. Names aren't unique (the friend code tells players apart), and an admin's rename doesn't count against the 14 days. Needs [migration 066](../supabase/migrations/066_display_name_changes.sql) after 065.

**Blocking and names:** tap someone in the Plaza (or use Friends → Recent, or a friend's options) to **Block** or **Report** them. Blocking ends the friendship, stops their requests and invites, and hides them from your Plaza; unblock under Friends → Recent. Player names go through a filter in the database (sign-up names that fail become "Trainer"). Needs [migration 110](../supabase/migrations/110_blocks_and_name_filter.sql) after 109; see [account setup](accounts-setup.md#blocking-players-and-the-name-filter).

**Deleting your account:** the bottom of your player card has _Delete account_; type DELETE to confirm. Your login and everything that's yours go for good; matches and raids you played stay for the others under "Deleted player". Administrators are refused (remove them in SQL). Needs [migration 109](../supabase/migrations/109_delete_my_account.sql) after 108; see [account setup](accounts-setup.md#deleting-an-account), which also covers turning on Sign in with Apple.

**After the match:** the result card shows a recap (damage dealt and taken, KOs, signature combos, MVP), your all-time record against each opponent, and a _Share result_ picture card. Matches count towards your records, ten achievements (each grants a title), fighter mastery badges on the roster and a monthly season leaderboard (menu → _Leaderboard_) once [migration 004](../supabase/migrations/004_matches_achievements.sql) is run; see [account setup](accounts-setup.md). Friends without an account can _Play as a guest_ (Supabase anonymous sign-ins must be switched on).

**Solo vs AI bot:** pick a difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard) under _Or play solo_ and press _Battle an AI bot_. You get a private best-of-five against a bot playing a random fighter. The bot runs on the server (`apps/server/src/bot.ts`) and sends the same inputs a player would, so cooldowns and rules apply to it unchanged. Difficulty sets its reaction delay, aim spread, target leading, dodging, skill use, how often it pauses its attacks, and whether it saves its ultimate for an opening. Simulated win rates against a newcomer-like player (late aim, misses, pauses) are about Easy 23%, Normal 48% and Hard 99%. Against a sharp player with steady aim they are about 1%, 5% and 76%. It walks around pillars, dodges big shots, jumps ground attacks, and can be fooled by decoys. Rematch is instant; _Back to menu_ leaves the room.

Default controls. Every key can be changed in **Settings → Controls & key bindings** (saved per browser), and each action has a backup key:

| Keyboard and mouse       | Controller (Xbox / PlayStation)        | Action                                  |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| W A S D (or arrows)      | Left stick or D-pad                    | Move                                    |
| Mouse                    | Right stick (centred: nearest foe)     | Aim                                     |
| Left mouse (hold)        | RT / R2 (hold)                         | Basic attack                            |
| Q / E / R (or 1 / 2 / 3) | X / Y / B (Square / Triangle / Circle) | Skills 1–3                              |
| F (or 4)                 | RB / R1                                | Ultimate (skill 4)                      |
| Right mouse (or Shift)   | LB or LT / L1 or L2                    | Dash                                    |
| Space                    | A / Cross                              | Jump (also "I'm ready" on the briefing) |
| Esc / Menu               | Start / Options                        | Preferences and leave room              |

Q, E, R and F sit next to W A S D, so you can cast without letting go of movement. **Aim preview** (the default) draws where a skill will land while you hold its key and casts when you let go; a quick tap still casts right away. Switch to **Instant** to cast on key press. **Aim assist** (off by default) snaps mouse basic attacks onto an opponent close to your cursor; skills always go exactly where you aim.

Mobile uses the left joystick and right skill/action buttons, with a separate bottom toolbar. Android/iOS PWA installation, safe-area layout and landscape play are retained. See [mobile/PWA setup](mobile-pwa.md). An App Store version wraps the same client with Capacitor (landscape-locked, native Sign in with Apple); see [the iPhone app](ios-app.md). Matches and sign-in require an internet connection.

## Boss Raid, Adventure and the Plaza

Co-op raids for 1–4 players open from the Raid Hub: a roster of seven bosses, three phases, breakable armor, stagger, revives, raid rites, Perils and cosmetic rewards including Victory celebrations. See [Boss Raid guide and deployment setup](boss-raid.md). Persistent rewards require the migrations through 028 and a server-only Supabase service-role key.

**Adventure:** the home screen’s Adventure card opens player levels 1–100, a free monthly seasonal reward journey, the Daily Spark and weekly arcade (Crown Chase, Meteor Mayhem and Ultimate Overload). XP comes from verified results and daily missions; rewards are cosmetic and automatic. Each level pays 30 coins, or 100 coins from level 50; titles come at levels 5, 30, 50, 60, 75, 90 and 100, and cosmetics every few levels in between, up to the Mythical Lv 55–100 set (Prism frame, Nova entrance, Starlit colors, Solar aura, Eclipse finale, Prismatic laurel and the Element Master frame). The Daily Spark doubles the first 500 XP each Pacific day. Seasons have 30 tiers of 1,200 XP (cosmetics at tiers 5, 12, 20 and 30) from the month after [migration 035](../supabase/migrations/035_level_100_daily_spark.sql) runs; earlier months keep 20 tiers of 400 XP. Arcade stays outside the rating ledger. Apply [migration 022](../supabase/migrations/022_player_journey.sql) after 021 for saved progression, migration 035 after 034 for levels 51–100, the Daily Spark and the 30-tier season, and [migration 051](../supabase/migrations/051_level_cosmetics.sql) after 050 for the Lv 55–100 cosmetics (it also gives them to everyone already past those levels). Apply [migration 028](../supabase/migrations/028_retire_raid_modes.sql) after 027 to retire Boss Rush and Raid of the Day while preserving earned rewards. See [the adventure guide](adventure-roadmap.md).

**Plaza:** the hub's _Plaza_ tab is a shared playground (up to 40 players per copy): walk, jump, dash, bounce pads, a stage with an LED wall, a ball pitch with four bots, a dash track with hurdles, a Photo Spot and the Volt Boba truck (free: 30% more speed for 30 s). A polaroid from the Photo Spot can be saved as a picture or kept in the Plaza's photo album (the _Album_ button), which holds the newest 30 on this device only (IndexedDB); nothing is uploaded. The game server moves everyone and runs the ball, lap times and the Orb Shower (the first minute of every quarter hour, UTC; caught orbs count toward the orbs quest). Everyone gets 10 free treats a Pacific day to throw (candy, cupcake, donut, lollipop, cookie). "Plaza today" has five quests paid automatically in shop coins once a Pacific day (goal 2, lap 2, cheers 2, orbs 1, photo 1), and TOP DASH shows the week's best laps. A player's equipped title shows over their name and on their card, in a look that grows with its tier (Common to Mythical, by how hard the title is to earn): plain coloured text, an ink plate, an edged plate with a glint, a gold-edged plate with sparks, a drifting ribbon. What moves plays for you and the five players nearest you, and holds still on Low graphics and with Reduce motion. Needs [migration 038](../supabase/migrations/038_plaza.sql) after 037.

**The Spire:** a climb in the Raid Hub for 1–4 players, seventy floors in seven zones, each with its own element. Each zone has nine floors of Echo waves (front-liners, casters and skirmishers), an elite on its fifth floor and, on its tenth, the Ascendant version of a raid boss in that boss's arena; clearing a zone's boss sets your checkpoint to the next zone. Data and how to add floors: [Updating the Spire](spire-content-guide.md) and `packages/shared/src/spire.ts`.

## Shop, coins and daily missions

**Shop, coins and daily missions:** raids, six daily missions, real matches against people (not bots) and the Plaza's daily quests pay coins, granted only by the game server with daily caps. The Shop (home screen, or the coin chip in the header) sells 144 champion skins in four rarities (Common, Rare, Epic, Mythical): eight for each of the eighteen champions, including a Cyber Mecha Epic ([migration 069](../supabase/migrations/069_cyber_mecha_epics.sql)); owning all eighteen Void Knights, all eighteen Cyber Mechas or all eight Arcade skins earns that collection's frame ([migrations 071](../supabase/migrations/071_collection_reward_frames.sql) and [072](../supabase/migrations/072_arcade_reward_frame.sql)) and six all-fighter collections (Colours, Aura, Entrance, Frame, Victory). Mythicals are the Void Knight set ([migration 067](../supabase/migrations/067_void_knight_mythicals.sql)), need a 30-win Mythic Trial with their champion and awaken in three stages as you win in them; everyone in the match sees your stage. Skins never change hitboxes, size or timing. Daily missions have their own screen (the home screen's _Daily missions_ card); players who only play alone get missions they can do alone ([migration 014](../supabase/migrations/014_missions.sql)). Needs [migration 010](../supabase/migrations/010_shop.sql), [011](../supabase/migrations/011_collection_bonus.sql) and, for the newest champions' skins, [053](../supabase/migrations/053_new_champion_skins.sql), [054](../supabase/migrations/054_new_champion_mythicals.sql), [070](../supabase/migrations/070_eighteen_champions.sql) and [071](../supabase/migrations/071_nature_void_knights_and_cyber.sql); design in [docs/raid-roster-design.md](raid-roster-design.md) §10.
