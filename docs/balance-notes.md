# Balance notes

The history of balance passes: new champions, the "every kit its own" and skill-revolution waves, and the health and attack-speed passes. Newest first. Moved here from the README (27 Nov 2026). The numbers in `packages/shared/src/index.ts` are the truth; these notes say why they are what they are. Back to the [README](../README.md).

**Eight new champions (November 2026).** Coilemur (Electric lemur, grappler), Pyranda (Fire red panda, artillery), Glacelle (Water sea angel, frost controller), Halcyra (Aura kingfisher, marksman), Sunbara (Light capybara, support), Astrabun (Psychic moon rabbit, blink duelist), Wombolder (Earth wombat, charger) and Shadopus (Shadow octopus, stealth assassin) join the roster, each with a basic attack, four skills, a signature combo, six rites and three raid champion tricks. They bring new mechanics: pulls, blinks, charges that slam into walls, stealth with an ambush bonus, healing springs and marksman shots that hit harder at range. Health and attack speed were then re-tuned for all sixteen together: bot win rates now run 47–52% (before: 42–60%). Kits, design and numbers are in [docs/new-champions](new-champions/README.md). Apply [migration 052](../supabase/migrations/052_sixteen_champions.sql) after 051, or choosing one of them as your portrait, or playing one, is refused. Each has seven Shop skins (migrations 053 and 054).

**Nature element: Pangrove and Mossloth (November 2026).** A ninth element brings the roster to eighteen, two per element again, and both play on mechanics no other champion has. **Pangrove** (a pangolin whose scales are living leaves, armoured burrower) wears three **armour plates** that each stop most of a big hit and grow back out of combat; it zips in on a **grappling tongue**, sheds its plates as a fan of blades (more plates, more blades), **tunnels underground** where nothing can hit it (its mound of dirt gives it away) and bursts up flinging enemies, and its ultimate whips its plates into a **storm of leaf blades** that circles it and bursts outward. **Mossloth** (a three-toed sloth with a moss garden on its back, time gardener) grows **moss while it stands still** (less damage taken, regeneration), plants **seeds that grow** from sprout to bloom and go off when stepped on, slows enemies and their shots in a **time bubble**, **swaps places** with an enemy or its own seedling, and its ultimate blooms every seedling at once. Each has a basic attack, four skills, a signature combo (Latched, Timelocked), six rites, three raid champion tricks, eight Shop skins (a Cyber Mecha Epic each; Void Knight Mythicals Thorn Warden and Blight Sentinel), illustrated icons and battle effects; the roster's overview explains each passive. Bot win rates and the design: [docs/nature-champions.md](nature-champions.md). Apply [migration 070](../supabase/migrations/070_eighteen_champions.sql) and then [071](../supabase/migrations/071_nature_void_knights_and_cyber.sql) after 069, or choosing one of them as your portrait, or playing one, is refused; they also give them their skins.

**Every kit its own, wave 1 (November 2026).** Seven skills that were copies of another champion's now play on mechanics no one else has. Tidefin's **Gill Siphon** locks a draining stream onto an enemy; Pyranda's **Ember Curtain** is a wall of embers that burns up enemy shots; Halcyra's **Halcyon Rewind** dives back to where it was two seconds ago and wins back some health, and its **Pinning Quill** leashes its target to where it was hit; Wombolder's **Bush Boomerang** hits on the way out and again on the way back, and its **Grudge Thump** taunts enemies into walking at it while it braces against knockback; Shadopus's **Ink Jet** blinds, so basic attacks miss. The waves still to come, the new mechanics and the balance: [docs/unique-kits.md](unique-kits.md).

**Every kit its own, wave 2 (November 2026).** Four of the six stun strikes and two of the four blast-ring ultimates now play differently. Lumoth's **Eyespot Flash** stuns only the enemies looking at it (look away to dodge it), and its **Radiant Flare** turns three rays of light round it like a lighthouse; Coilemur's **Static Clamp** fixes a charge on its target that goes off a moment later and arcs to enemies nearby; Astrabun's **Mochi Pound** is a chain of three pounds; Shadopus's **Kraken Grasp** seizes an enemy and hurls it where you aim (harder into a wall); Auralynx's **Shard Nova** became **Crystal Cage**, a ring of crystal no one and no shot can cross. Shatter Paw, Shell Bash, Overcharge and Tremor keep their roles. Details and balance: [docs/unique-kits.md](unique-kits.md).

**Every kit its own, wave 3 (November 2026).** The slow zones and the "big circle at the aim" ultimates that copied each other now play differently. Tidefin's **Undertow** is a current that sweeps enemies along your aim (jump out of it); Astrabun's **Moon Gravity** makes enemies float, sliding with almost no grip; Coilemur's **Spark Trail** speeds it up and leaves a crackling trail behind it; Cindrake's **Sunfire Pillar** follows your aim for 3 seconds; Mystowl's **Mind Maze** confuses enemies, reversing every way they walk. Mud Mire and Chestnut Storm keep their roles. Details and balance: [docs/unique-kits.md](unique-kits.md).

**Skill revolution, wave A (November 2026).** Skills that did little for their champion, and ultimates that only hurt up close, are replaced, starting with the four that needed it most ([design](skill-revolution.md)). Tidefin's **River Dash** shoots it through enemies on a jet of water (go through someone and it is ready again in 3 seconds), and its **Riptide Wave** is now a wall of water as wide as a quarter of the court that rolls across all of it, carrying enemies into the walls (jump it). Voltkit's **Ball Lightning** flies through enemies, zapping whoever is near, and its new ultimate **Thunderstorm** drops three bolts on the enemy nearest its aim, anywhere on the court (keep changing direction). Duskveil's **Shade Crawl** roots, and its new ultimate **Wraith Hunt** sends a wraith that hunts you through walls and takes you if you are badly hurt (run). Lumoth's **Radiant Flare** hangs like a little sun at the aim, draws enemies in, then bursts and stuns (dash out).

**Skill revolution, wave B (November 2026).** Five more kits get a skill that pulls its weight ([design](skill-revolution.md)). Cindrake's **Magma Leap** leaps over ground attacks and lands in an eruption that burns. Pyranda's **Chestnut Storm** creeps along the aim in five marked blasts, over walls (step out of the line). Mossloth's **Overgrowth** grows a garden at the aim that slows, and roots whoever is still on it at the end. Mystowl's **Hush** silences everyone close for 1.2 seconds: no skills. Astrabun's **Sticky Mochi** grounds the first enemy it hits: no dash, no jump. Cindrake's fire also costs older phones much less to draw.

**Skill revolution, wave C (November 2026).** Glacelle's Hailstone becomes **Black Ice** ([design](skill-revolution.md)): a patch of ice at the aim where enemies slide with almost no grip, so they can't step out of Frost Bloom's freeze in time. The other five champions in the wave (Halcyra, Sunbara, Coilemur, Shadopus, Auralynx) already use every skill, so their kits stay as they are; Normal bots were retuned so each lands near half its duels.

**Creature remodel (November 2026).** Each of the original eight kits was rebuilt around its creature's real animal (Static Glide, Shell Up, Tide Double, Eyespot Flash, Haunted Lantern; the rest renamed), and basic attacks now play at their stated speed (ticks used to round every interval up, costing up to 10%). The kits were tested against the eight new champions being designed, then health and attack speed were re-tuned for the eight that ship: bot win rates now run 48–54% (before: 45–54%). Details and the new numbers are in [docs/creature-remodel.md](creature-remodel.md).

**November 2026 pass (health and basic-attack speed).** Bot round robins on the server simulation (every pairing, 40 games each, Hard and Normal bots, sides swapped every game) showed the fighters had drifted apart. Averaged over both bot settings, win rates ran from 24% (Mystowl) to 73% (Terradon). Each fighter's maximum health and basic-attack interval were tuned toward 50% over repeated rounds of matches. Damage, cooldowns, skills and rite texts are unchanged, and changes stay within about a quarter so no fighter changes role.

Win rates are the average of Hard and Normal bots, fighting only each other. The two settings still disagree for some fighters: Cindrake wins 32% against Hard bots, which dodge, but 71% against Normal ones; Mystowl (73% / 36%) and Duskveil (36% / 64%) split the other way. One strength number can only centre the average, so those gaps need kit changes and human playtesting. Health and attack speed also apply in raids, so raid difficulty for these fighters shifts slightly.

The earlier durability pass increased every fighter’s maximum HP by 25% (rounded to whole HP) and Hibernate healing from 60 to 75 HP (Hibernate is now Shell Up). Damage, cooldowns and sleep duration are unchanged. The earlier win-rate results below predate this pass and need fresh playtesting.

Hibernate (since replaced by Shell Up) then got a price: while Terradon slept it took 20% more damage (`STATUS_RULES.sleepVulnerability`), and the HUD shows `SLEEP +20% DMG` so the opponent knows to punish. In 168-match simulations per style, a Terradon that sleeps carelessly mid-fight took about 12% more damage and its win rate fell from 24% to 17%. A Hard bot Terradon that only sleeps when safe was unaffected (72%).

October 2026 pass, tuned with thousands of simulated matches (simple human-like players and Hard bots, every fighter pairing) until every fighter averaged 44–59% wins (before: 10–96%):

- Basic attacks knock back lightly by default (20), so ranged darts no longer shove brawlers away; melee basics barely push (Cindrake 10, Auralynx 12, Terradon 25) so they can chain hits.
- Brawlers hit harder and reach further: Cinder Snap 17 dmg / 170 range, Prism Jab 15 / 130, Boulder Swipe 18 / 150; Ember Gust 6 per tick; Terradon speed 222.
- Mystowl toned down: 104 HP, Mind Dart 9, Focus Ray 18, Thoughtstrike 38.
- Duskveil 120 HP and Lantern Wisp 9; Glint 6; Tide Needle range 640.

**Longer matches (Oct 2026):** every mode is now best of five (first to three round wins). Healing orbs appear 12 s into each round and every 18 s after (one at a time in 1v1, alternating top and bottom centre; both spots in 2v2); the closest hurt fighter who touches one regains 25% of full health, and full-health fighters walk through them. Basic attacks were trimmed (Spark Dart 6, Cinder Snap 15, Tide Needle 5, Prism Jab 13, Glint 6, Mind Dart 8, Boulder Swipe 15, Lantern Wisp 9) so skills and positioning matter more. Simulated fighters stay within 47–53% average win rates.

Fighter cards show **Long range** or **Close range** instead of the basic attack; the basic attack's details stay in the panel below the roster.
