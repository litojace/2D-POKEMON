## Real-time schedule

- Seed shop restocks every 4 h at 2:00, 6:00, 10:00 …; market prices change at midnight; weather every 4 h at 0:00, 4:00 ….
- Crows try to land roughly every 1–2 h (a crow eats a ripe crop 5 s after landing unless shooed, a dog or a scarecrow is there); friends visit roughly every 2–4 h.
- A skipped order is replaced after 5 minutes. Cow (pasture, Lv 9): milk every 5 h 20 m.
- Plaza Orb Shower: first minute of every :00 :15 :30 :45; day/night follows the local clock.
