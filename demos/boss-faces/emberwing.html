How a feature is placed

  1. The head's yaw picks the drawing: front, three-quarter (a near and a far eye), side, then no face. Left turns mirror the right-turn drawings; the front drawing and the eye highlights are never mirrored.
  2. Height is the feature's line and never changes with the view. A nod slides all of them together.
  3. Across: an eye is at eyeOrbit × sin(faceYaw ∓ eyeAz); features on the centre line are at centreLine(height) × sin(faceYaw), where centreLine is the head's own profile. faceYaw = yaw − lag × sin(2 × yaw), so the face trails the skull a little.
  4. The far eye may hide at most tuckCap of its width behind the bridge. Then each feature is clamped inside the drawn outline of the current view by its own half-width plus a margin.
  5. Everything turns by the head's roll only. The far eye adds one fixed lift of its outer corner.
  6. Draw order: head, far eye, the occluder, near eye, the other features, highlights.
