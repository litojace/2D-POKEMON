Gloomshade face rig

Every piece is drawn once; each frame only moves, turns and re-sorts them. The eyes, brows, nose, mouth and gem each have a front, three-quarter (near and far) and side drawing, placed on one centre line and one eye line.

How a feature is placed

  1. The head's yaw picks the drawing: front under 22.5°, three-quarter to 67.5°, side to 112.5°, no face beyond. Left turns mirror the right-turn drawings; the eye highlights are never mirrored.
  2. Height is the feature's line (gem, brow, eye, nose) and never changes with the view. A nod slides all of them together.
  3. Across: an eye is at eyeOrbit × sin(faceYaw ± eyeAz); the gem, nose and mouth are at centreLine(height) × sin(faceYaw), where centreLine is the head's own profile. faceYaw = yaw − lag × sin(2 × yaw), so the face trails the skull a little.
  4. Each feature is clamped inside the drawn outline of the current view by its own half-width plus a margin.
  5. Everything turns by the head's roll only. The far eye adds one fixed lift of its outer corner.
  6. Draw order: head, far eye, muzzle (its far edge tucks the far eye's inner corner), near eye, gem, mouth, nose, highlights.
