Ways to play

Duel, team up or take on a giant

  • Duels

    1 v 1

    Best of five rounds. In the last minute a ring closes to the centre, so nobody can run forever.

  • 2v2 Teams

    2 v 2

    Stay close to your partner: each element gives a team-up gift, from faster skills to healing.

  • Free-for-all

    3–4 players

    Everyone against everyone. The last fighter standing takes the round.

  • Raids

    1–4 players

    Learn a boss's tricks and beat it together, for outfits, auras and victory poses.

  • The Spire

    70 floors

    Seven zones of ten floors, with an elite halfway up and a boss at the top of each.

  • The Plaza

    Hang out

    Meet other players between matches, show off your look and send a friend invite.

Create a private room and share its six-character code, or practise against bots. House rules like Turbo, Giants and Orb Rush change things up.

Raid bosses

Seven bosses, seven puzzles

Every boss has one trick to learn. Solve it with up to three friends. Start with Cragmuff: one clear opens the other six.

Start hereEarth · 13 rewards

★★★

Cragmuff

The crystal-horned ram

A grumpy, stubborn mountain ram in a coat of mossy stone wool, with curled horns of glowing amber crystal. Lead its charges into pillars to knock it dizzy and strike the crystal core on its chest.

How to beat it

Bait its charge into a pillar

Water · 11 rewards

★★★

Tidelotl

The rising tide

A mischievous axolotl who calls itself lord of the tides, with a pearl on its brow. Lure its dive onto dry land, share the islands when the tide rises, and pop your friends out of bubbles.

How to beat it

Strand it on an island

Shadow · 11 rewards

★★★

Inkpurr

Lights out

A round, cat-eared sprite of living ink with a crescent-moon tail. Keep the lanterns lit, find the real one among its copies, and spread out when the curse creeps.

How to beat it

Find the one that casts a shadow

Psychic · 11 rewards

★★★

Snoozaku

The dream-eating baku

A chubby, sleepy baku in a starry nightcap that snacks on dreams. One raider sees its dream; the team must step the runes in order to wake it.

How to beat it

Step the runes only the Dreamer sees

Light · 11 rewards

★★★

Prismell

The prism-shelled snail

A proud, sparkly snail in a spiral shell of prism crystal that splits the light into rainbows. Its heart glows in the coil behind a shield: pass the sunbeam raider to raider until it reaches the heart and shatters the shield.

How to beat it

Relay the sunbeam to its heart

Fire · 11 rewards

★★★

Lumiwick

Ground the sky

A smug sky-lantern spirit that refuses to come down. Light all three braziers together to pull it out of the sky.

How to beat it

Light three braziers at once

Electric · 11 rewards

★★★

Storm Twins

Fizz & Crackle

Two floating thunder-jellies tied by a crackling ribbon. Pair up with opposite charges and bring both down before either can revive the other.

How to beat it

Beat both twins together
