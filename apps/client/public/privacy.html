Privacy Policy

Element Arena · Effective November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information Element Arena (the game at parena.litojace.com and the Element Arena app for iPhone) collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Element Arena is run by Jacen Nguyen, an individual developer, as litojace ("we", "us").

  • We collect only what the game needs to run your account and matches.
  • No ads, no advertising trackers, no analytics services, and we never sell your information.
  • Other players see your player name, level, title and looks, and your friends see when you're online.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, inside the game.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address and password, if you sign up with email. Your password is stored by our sign-in provider (Supabase) in a form we can't read.
  • Sign in with Apple: the email address and name Apple shares with us. With Apple's "Hide My Email", we get a private relay address instead of your real one. In the iPhone app, we also keep a token from Apple so we can disconnect Sign in with Apple when you delete your account.
  • Guest play: no email at all; we keep an anonymous account and the nickname you choose.
  • Your player name, and a friend code made from your account's ID.

Your games

  • Match results (who played, which fighters, rounds won, damage, knockouts), your rating and rank, raid results, achievements and titles.
  • Your progress: levels, missions, in-game coins and items (these are earned in the game, never bought with money), your farm and bistro, the fighters and looks you've chosen, and your settings that are saved to your account.

Friends and other players

  • Your friends, friend requests, and the players you've blocked.
  • When you were last online, and, while you play, what you're doing (for example in a match or in the Plaza) so your friends can join or watch.
  • Reports you send us: the category and message you write, the screen and room you were in, your device type, screen size and browser, and the player you're reporting, if any.

Technical information

  • Connection logs: when a game connection drops or fails, our game server writes a short technical log (the reason, the room, and your account ID) so we can fix network problems.
  • Performance reports: after a match, the game sends frame rates, slowest frames, graphics level, device memory and number of processor cores. These reports don't include your account.
  • Load times: when a battle starts, the game sends how long it took to load, with your device memory and number of processor cores. These are logged with your account ID, like connection logs, so we can fix slow loading.
  • IP addresses: our game server uses your IP address for a moment to stop too many requests from one place, without storing it. Our hosting providers keep standard request logs, which include IP addresses.

On your device

The game saves your settings (controls, sound, graphics), whether to keep you signed in, and some game data on your device using your browser's storage. The website also keeps a copy of the game's files so it loads faster. None of this is used for advertising or tracking, and we don't use cookies to track you.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To play matches and raids, keep your progress, ratings and rewards, and run friends, the Plaza and leaderboards.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: stopping cheating and abuse, filtering player names, acting on reports, and honoring blocks.
  • To find and fix bugs, connection problems and slow performance.
  • To email you about your account (confirming your email, resetting your password). We don't send marketing email.

What other players can see

  • Your player name, level, title, fighter and looks: in matches, in the Plaza, on leaderboards and on match results.
  • Your match records against them (wins and losses), on their results and recent players.
  • Your friends also see whether you're online, when you were last online, and whether you're in a room they can join or watch.
  • Other players never see your email address.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We use these services to run the game, and they handle information only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in and the game's database (United States).
  • Render: hosting the website and the game server (United States).
  • Apple, only if you choose to sign in with Apple. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that sign-in.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect players and the game from fraud, abuse or harm.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account and game data until you delete your account.
  • Guest accounts live only on the device you play on: if you log out of a guest account without saving it with an email, it can't be recovered.
  • Connection logs and our hosting providers' request logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted.
  • Our providers may keep backups for a limited time after something is deleted.

Deleting your account

Open your player card (tap your name at the top of the screen), choose Delete account at the bottom, and type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your login, profile, progress, items, friends and blocks.

  • Matches and raids you played stay in the other players' history, with you shown as "Deleted player" and without your account ID or name.
  • Reports you sent are kept for our moderators, without your name.
  • If you signed in with Apple in the iPhone app, deleting your account also disconnects Sign in with Apple. If you signed in with Apple on the website, you can disconnect it yourself in your Apple Account settings, under Sign in with Apple.

If you can't sign in, contact us at privacy@litojace.com and we'll delete your account for you.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your player name on your player card (once every 14 days).
  • Block players, and report players or problems, from the game.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing privacy@litojace.com. Depending on where you live (for example the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have these rights by law; we honor these requests for everyone.

Children

Element Arena isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Before anyone creates an account or plays as a guest, the game asks for their month and year of birth, and doesn't let anyone under 13 create an account. We keep only whether the person can create an account, on their device, not the date. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us at privacy@litojace.com and we'll delete it.

Security

All connections to the game are encrypted (HTTPS). Account data is stored with access limited to the game's own server, and players can only reach their own information through the game. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the game if the change is significant.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@litojace.com.
