Open beta

What does "open beta" mean?

Element Arena is live and free for everyone to play. We're still adding to it and polishing it, so you'll see new things and fixes often, and now and then something that isn't finished yet.

Is my progress kept?

Yes. Your progress is saved to your account on our servers and carries over through every update. Ranked ratings move a quarter of the way back toward 1000 at the start of each month; your best rating and titles stay.

A guest account lives only on its device: after you log out or clear your browser's data, it can't be recovered. To keep it, open your player card and add your email under Keep your progress.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

In the game, open Menu → Report a problem (it's in Settings too) and pick Bug or glitch, or Something else for ideas and feedback. It tells us which screen you were on. You can also email privacy@elementarenausa.com.

Is there an iPhone or Android app?

Not yet: the apps are on their way. Until then, play in your phone's browser at play.elementarenausa.com and add it to your home screen.

Playing

How do I play a match on my own?

Pick a bot level and choose Battle an AI bot. It plays a full match against the computer, no other players needed.

How do the controls work?

Open Menu → How to play. On a phone, the big button is your basic attack and the four buttons around it are your skills. You can change them in Settings → Controls & key bindings.

How do I chat, or message a friend?

Tap the chat button beside your player card. Its tabs are your party, your room or the players near you, and Friends: pick a friend to message them, or choose Message in their options in your Friends list. Messages between friends are kept (the last 200), so they can read them later even if they were offline. In a battle, the chat button top left sends quick calls and emotes to your team.

Are coins bought with money?

No. Coins, skins and items are earned by playing. Element Arena has no real-money purchases.

Your account

I forgot my password

On the log-in screen, choose Forgot password? and enter your email address. We'll email you a link to choose a new password. This also works if you made your account with Google before Google sign-in was removed: use the email address of that Google account.

How do I change my player name?

Tap your name at the top of the screen to open your player card. You can change your name once every 14 days.

How do I delete my account?

Open your player card (tap your name at the top of the screen), choose Delete account at the bottom, and type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your account and its data. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll delete it for you. See the privacy policy for what's kept.

Safety

How do I report a player or a problem?

Use Menu → Report a problem, or Report on a player in the Plaza. In chat, tap the message and choose Report: the last messages go with it. We read every report, and players who are reported a lot can't type in chat for a while.

How do I stop seeing someone's chat, or chat at all?

Tap their message and choose Mute (until you restart the game) or Block. With the gear in the chat you can keep chat on, use quick chat only (preset phrases and emotes), or turn it off. In a battle, Mute rivals hides the other team's emotes.

How do I block someone?

Choose Block on them in the Plaza or in your Friends list, or on a chat message. They leave your friends, can't send you requests, invites or chat, and disappear from your Plaza.
