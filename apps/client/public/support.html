Support

Element Arena · Help and contact

Need help? Email privacy@litojace.com and tell us your player name or friend code and what happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

Playing

How do I play a match on my own?

Pick a bot level and choose Battle an AI bot. It plays a full match against the computer, no other players needed.

How do the controls work?

Open Menu → How to play. On a phone, the big button is your basic attack and the four buttons around it are your skills. You can change them in Settings → Controls & key bindings.

Are coins bought with money?

No. Coins, skins and items are earned by playing. Element Arena has no real-money purchases.

Your account

I forgot my password

On the log-in screen, choose Forgot password? and enter your email address. We'll email you a link to choose a new password. This also works if you made your account with Google before Google sign-in was removed: use the email address of that Google account.

How do I change my player name?

Tap your name at the top of the screen to open your player card. You can change your name once every 14 days.

How do I delete my account?

Open your player card (tap your name at the top of the screen), choose Delete account at the bottom, and type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your account and its data. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll delete it for you. See the privacy policy for what's kept.

Safety

How do I report a player or a problem?

Use Menu → Report a problem, or Report on a player in the Plaza. We read every report.

How do I block someone?

Choose Block on them in the Plaza or in your Friends list. They leave your friends, can't send you requests or invites, and disappear from your Plaza.
